# A Project for my Major

## Zotero

### 1. About the project 

Zotero is a free tool for research citation. It allows users to collect, save and cite sources directly from websites and  easily add them in a document. The tool was created at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University.

### 2. Connection to my major & example

As my major Intercultural Studies uses a lot of sources from websites, research papers and books, Zotero helps by efficiently saving these with one click through the browser extension. With that the time-consuming manual writing of sources in documents is not necessary anymore.

### 4. Your verdict

I would and will rely on Zotero for my thesis and have already started to collect sources across different topics in my Zotero library. This means that I am somewhat dependent on the volunteer maintainers of Zotero. If I would decide to leave Zotero, I would be able to export my library of collected sources and therefore not lose my collected materials.
