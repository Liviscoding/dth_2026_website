# A Project for my Major

## Zotero

1.	What the project is, what it does, and who made it?

Zotero is a free tool for research citation. It allows users to collect, save and cite sources directly from websites and add easily in a document. The tool was created at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University.

2.	How it would help in your major, with one concrete example task?

Zotero helps by efficiently saving sources with one click through the browser extension. With that the time-consuming manual writing of sources in documents is not necessary anymore

    5.	Money: who funds it? It was initially funded from the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation, the United States Institute of Museum and Library Services, and the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation. Now it is a project of the nonprofit „Corporation for Digital Scholarship”

4.	Your verdict: would you rely on it for a thesis? Could you leave it again if you had to? 

Yes, I would and will rely on Zotero for my thesis and have already started collecting sources across different topics in my Zotero library. This means that I am dependent on the volunteer maintainers of Zotero. Yes, I could leave Zotero if I had to, thanks to the possibility of exporting my library, I wouldn’t lose my collected materials
