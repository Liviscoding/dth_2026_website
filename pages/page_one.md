# Advantages of Open Source Software

## Resisting Capitalism

From a **macro perspective**, one of the main advantages that came to my mind while learning about open source software is the resistance of ownership and patenting knowledge. In a society, where ideas, designs and even seeds get patented, making hours of working on code available to everyone, feels *political and anti-capitalistic* to me. This counter-behavior is an advantage in itself in my perspective, as it gives access to everyone, no matter their income or hometown. In comparison, for example Apple’s ecosystem not being open source, generates profit by having a closed and non-transparent code. 

## Community & Purpose

Additionally, I see the community aspect of open source software as a big advantage. One reason for that is the collaboration of many *engaged people across the planet* creating a swarm intelligence. Through that, people with different backgrounds, perspectives and knowledge levels can find different problems and can find solutions for them. This collaboration of volunteer maintainers can create a sense of shared purpose while their work helps **identifying vulnerabilities** that could be exploited.

## Education

Furthermore, open source software can work as a training and education for a person learning about coding, such as a student. Being able to read, modify code and research fixes as well as new features by other volunteers, gives access to free teaching materials. Once the learning increases, doing small fixes can be a way to build up skills step by step. 

## Saving Taxes

From a municipality’s point of view, having access to code and similar applications can save tax money. This is because the software doesn’t have to be developed *from scratch every time*.
