# Disadvantages of Open Source Software

## Volunteer’s responsibility

Being a volunteer myself in a different context, I know that there exists a flipside of the coin. One is the timing, volunteer work often takes places after a day at work or university, which can mean being tired and preoccupied with stress from a long day. This can impact awareness, patience and mental capacity for the volunteering. Additionally, the fact of not being remunerated for the work of maintaining, fixing and checking code can lead to being unmotivating and present reasons for people to stop showing up. 

## Misuse of access

The fact that everyone can change the code gives people, corporations and governments the possibility to misuse the system. The incident of the volunteer “Jia Tan” in the open source “XZ Utils” is an example of this loophole of gaining trust by actually fixing and ending up misusing the open source system for their own benefit. 

## License complexity

Being a new student to coding myself, I could see the possibility of misunderstanding or potentially misusing licenses of open source software. This could have negative consequences and could be a liability. Therefore, I see it this complexity a disadvantage for newcomers in the open source ecosystem.
